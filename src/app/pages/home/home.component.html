How Athena Works

One goal in. A living, adaptive learning path out. Athena runs the loop so you only have to show up.

01

Describe Your Goal

"Become a backend engineer." "Pass system design interviews." Plain language is enough — Athena does the rest.

02

AI Assesses Your Skills

A short adaptive diagnostic maps what you already know, where you're shaky, and how you learn best.

03

Athena Creates Your Roadmap

A phased, personalized path with milestones, projects, and checkpoints — sequenced for your starting point.

04

Learn & Adapt

Every session, quiz, and interview feeds the engine. Your roadmap reshapes itself around your weaknesses.
